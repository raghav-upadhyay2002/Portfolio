Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This is a personal portfolio site. It doesn't have user accounts, doesn't run ads, and doesn't sell or share data with anyone. Here's exactly what does happen when you visit.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics (GA4) to see aggregate traffic: which pages get visited, roughly where from, what device and browser, and which link brought you here. Google Analytics sets cookies and processes an IP-derived approximate location to do this. I don't receive your name, email, or any other way to identify you individually from it, just aggregate numbers. See Google's Privacy Policy for how Google itself handles that data.

Information you send directly

If you email me or message me on LinkedIn/GitHub using the links on this site, I receive whatever you choose to send (your address, name, and message). That's used only to reply to you and isn't stored anywhere beyond my own inbox, and isn't shared with anyone else.

Cookies

The only cookies set on this site are the ones Google Analytics uses for the purpose above. There are no advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

Third-party links

This site links out to things like GitHub, Hugging Face Spaces, arXiv, and LinkedIn. Once you click through, you're on their site under their privacy policy, not this one.

Questions

Email raghav0408upadhyay@gmail.com with any questions about this policy or to request that analytics data associated with your visit be disregarded.
