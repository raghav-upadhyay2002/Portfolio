## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a living preprint, not a resume page — it refuses both the dashboard/glassmorphism AI-portfolio default and the cream-paper editorial default, proving rigor through the literal conventions of a scholarly manuscript instead of describing it.

OWN-WORLD: Dark graphite/near-black ground (a PDF read at night, not paper on a desk); off-white manuscript ink; one functional accent, a non-repro blue, for marks/links/figure numbers; a reviewer-red reserved only for gate/fail states. Source Serif 4 for manuscript display and body prose, Inter at UI sizes only (nav/chrome/labels), JetBrains Mono for data, figure numbers, captions, timestamps. Components: title block, running paper-header nav (replaces navbar), numbered Figures with real captions, inline citation-footnotes, results tables, a revision-history timeline, an Appendix, a Correspondence section.

STORY: A hiring manager or research collaborator arrives already skeptical of AI-portfolio flash; the manuscript register argues "measured, not eyeballed" before a word is read; they leave able to cite one specific figure or number as their reason to email him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A running arXiv-style identifier bar at the top; a title block (name set as the paper title, tagline as the thesis statement, affiliation line beneath); a true Abstract paragraph beside a metadata/status panel (availability, key stats rendered as footnoted figures, not stat-card counters); a live monospace build-log ticker strip anchoring the fold.

FORM: The Manuscript — academic preprint/journal world, grounded candidate 5 of 7, assigned by the roll over my top-ranked Build Log candidate; seed key ce8515b5. Raises folded in: staged escalating-proof pacing (declined Deco-cinema-lobby donation), inspectable click-to-expand citations/metrics (declined HyperCard donation), a live monospace log-strip preserving engineering legibility (competitive Phosphor-Terminal donation), a single-gesture pipeline-diagram reveal (declined transforming-cape donation), a literal illumination/degradation motif on the robotics figure tied to the real dimming-light finding (declined Daylight-Section donation), sparing marginal check-mark trust signals on independently-verifiable claims (declined club-sleeve donation, defacement itself dropped).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
